FAQ

Questions about PriceWatcher.

The short version: add a competitor pricing URL, choose how often it should be checked, and PriceWatcher records meaningful pricing-related changes.

What exactly does PriceWatcher monitor?

A specific public URL that contains a competitor's pricing, plans, packaging, or related commercial information. Each URL is one monitor.

How is this different from a generic website-change monitor?

PriceWatcher is intentionally focused on pricing. It filters for plan names, currencies, billing periods, usage limits, seats, credits, and other pricing-related language before comparing snapshots, which reduces unrelated page noise.

How often can pages be checked?

The current app supports hourly, every 6 hours, every 12 hours, daily, and weekly monitoring.

What happens when a change is detected?

PriceWatcher records added and removed pricing-related lines, stores the event in history, and attempts to classify the change. When possible it produces a concise summary such as “$29 → $39.”

Can PriceWatcher notify other tools?

Yes. Each monitor can send a webhook when pricing changes. That can connect to Slack, Discord, Zapier, Make, n8n, or your own HTTPS endpoint.

Does it support email alerts?

Webhook alerts are available now. Email alerts are not currently included; connect a webhook to Slack, Discord, Zapier, Make, n8n, or your own notification workflow.

Will it work on every pricing page?

No crawler can guarantee every site. The current crawler works best on server-rendered public pages. Heavily JavaScript-rendered pages or sites with strong anti-bot protections may eventually require a browser-rendering crawler.

Does PriceWatcher use AI to invent or guess pricing changes?

No. The core detection is deterministic and based on stored page snapshots. Change summaries are derived from the actual added and removed pricing text; the raw evidence remains visible.

Can I pause a monitor?

Yes. Pausing stops scheduled checks while preserving the monitor's snapshots and change history.

Can I export my data?

Yes. The dashboard can export monitoring and change data as JSON.

Is billing live?

Yes. Free accounts are available, and Pro and Business subscriptions use Stripe-hosted Checkout. Customers can manage payment methods, invoices, and cancellations through the Stripe customer portal.

Is PriceWatcher publicly available?

Yes. Anyone can create an account and start with the Free plan. The monitoring dashboard requires a verified PriceWatcher account.
