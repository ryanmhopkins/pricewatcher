From page change to market signal

Competitive intelligence without the manual patrol.

A generic page monitor tells you pixels moved. PlanSentry focuses on the commercial structure of the page—and preserves the evidence behind every alert.

01 · Capture

Build a reliable baseline

Add a public pricing URL. PlanSentry captures its current plans, price points, packaging signals, annual offers, and a private visual snapshot.

02 · Understand

Classify what changed

Separate price moves, plan launches, feature removals, limit changes, and discount shifts from routine page noise.

03 · Act

Route the signal

Review the change feed, compare history, set smart thresholds, or send alerts by email and webhook.
