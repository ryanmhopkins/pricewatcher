Pricing intelligence, end to end

Track the offer. Understand the move.

PlanSentry watches the commercial signals competitors publish—their prices, plans, packages, limits, features, and promotions—and preserves a defensible record of what changed.

01
Monitor

Reliable scheduled capture

Monitor public pricing URLs hourly, every 6 or 12 hours, daily, or weekly according to your plan. Pause and resume without losing history, run manual checks, and see crawler health at a glance.

02
Structure

Plan and price extraction

Convert readable pricing content into detected plans, amounts, currencies, and billing periods so the current offer is understandable before a change ever occurs.

03
Package

Limits and feature intelligence

Track storage, seats, users, credits, tokens, usage allowances, support levels, and included features. Added and removed package signals become structured change evidence.

04
Offers

Annual discount tracking

Identify yearly billing offers, advertised savings, months-free promotions, effective monthly price, and material changes to annual incentives.

05
Compare

History, screenshots, and matrix views

Follow plan prices over time, compare private visual snapshots, inspect raw diffs, and scan multiple competitors from one pricing matrix.

06

Alerts built around importance

Send immediate, daily, or weekly email notifications; connect webhooks; filter by severity; and trigger smart thresholds for price moves, free-tier removals, or enterprise launches.
